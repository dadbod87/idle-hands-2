# UO music follow-up — no asset import

Primary sources inspected November18:
- EA current content policy: https://help.ea.com/en/articles/security-and-rules/ea-content-policy/ . Its personal fan-content rules do not establish clearance to combine game content with an independent third-party game, and do not grant third-party music rights. No permission form/message sent.
- Original ORIGIN Soundtrack booklet scan, page2: https://www.mocagh.org/origin/originsoundtrackv1-manual.pdf . The historical Stones credit names David Watson/Kathleen Jones and notes permission for that release. It is evidence of historical credits, not a license for Idle Hands2, proof of current ownership, or clearance of the particular UO MIDI arrangement/soundfont/recording.

No exact reusable license was found in this bounded follow-up. This is not a claim that one cannot be obtained. Secondary fan pages and older discussions were leads only; their ownership assertions were not treated as current authority. No music/MIDI/sheet transcription/recording was downloaded, copied or imported; no third-party melody was added to the accepted original sketches. Keep original129music available while exact UO rights remain unresolved. Do not invent an asset license or treat a public download as permission. No asset spending or contact authorization inferred.

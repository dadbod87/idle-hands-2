# Owner direction and design boundaries

Captured from the 2026-11-10 conversation after IH2-051. This is a synthesized decision record, not a verbatim transcript. Root [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md) records formal IDs; this page keeps the larger discussion discoverable. Future details remain unimplemented unless the task evidence says otherwise.

| Owner direction | Design implication |
|---|---|
| Art for every item and buildable | Track icons, placed/equipped forms, stages and action poses before content is called finished. |
| Opening celebrations and door animations | Cosmetic, replay-safe, reduced-motion alternatives; retain authoritative doorway confirmation. |
| Materials plus build/opening timers | A real server-owned construction stage, clear completion time and useful alternate work while waiting. |
| Overhead chat and timestamped message journal | Bubbles are transient presentation; journal entries retain channel, sender and server time. |
| Private messages as appropriate | Separate participant-authorized conversations; no public bubble leakage. |
| Forests between towns, monsters and rare resources | Regional gathering/exploration choices and optional danger; preserve useful safe town play. |
| Small dungeons, weapons and food | Separate combat/equipment/consumption/loot contracts with distinct art and gestures. |
| Different cities and housing locations | Earned services/materials/visual identity and persistent address/plot ownership. |
| Help, resource sharing, buffs and friendly sabotage/jokes | Atomic consent-aware help and bounded reversible mischief, adapted from 1.0's spirit. |
| Health bars; death returns to a town bed; temporary corpse loot | Server-owned combat/death/recovery state, clear countdown and reachable recovery route. Exact drop/expiry rules are not settled. |
| Character design, name and backstory before waking in bed | A short opening, temporary lodging distinct from an earned home, and identity saved before world entry. |
| Keep it free and build carefully with little upfront input | Original/verified free assets, staged prototypes, recorded reversible defaults and no new billed/public commitments. |

## Accepted versus recommended

The humorous second teaser was owner-approved. Its caption is **“New town. Same questionable work ethic.”** with **“Build. Explore. Try to look busy.”** and a concept-art disclosure. This accepts a marketing illustration, not a complete production art library or a promise that all depicted systems are ready.

Browser-first, installable PWA, optional packaged clients later is the agent's recommendation after the owner asked about website/download delivery. Keep that recommendation separate from a shipped install feature or an irreversible engine decision.

The temporary rental-bed opening, flavor-only initial backstories, safe-town crafting alternative to combat, first public Town chat, opt-out/consent controls for pranks, protected essentials, candidate city names and a 30-minute corpse experiment are agent-selected defaults for review. They should be explicit and testable, not presented as exact owner instructions.

The owner did not finalize which equipped items drop, what happens at corpse expiry, exact construction waits, combat balance, chat retention, property limits or regional prices. Make prototypes/data comparisons first; an unresolved release policy is not a reason to stop safe independent design work or ask a large questionnaire now.

## Continuity boundary

The original game's accounts, rules and art do not silently carry over. Current sequel workshop/bridge/plot proof remains intact. New ideas are design input for future slices, not permission to mark combat, shops, chat, native packaging or multiple cities implemented. Preserve persistent homes and creative work through weekly competition. Keep private-message authorization and real accounts behind their actual implementation/security gates.

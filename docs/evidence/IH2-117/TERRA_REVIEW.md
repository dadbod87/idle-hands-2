Keep the ownership count visible when names are long, perhaps by placing the icon above or outside the selector or showing the count separately. The separate distribution line is currently readable.

The large desktop surround and small centered room make the furnishings feel distant; consider responsive scene scale in a later visual pass. The saved phone room is more legible, though the upper scene label overlaps the bed and clips some room content at the edges.
- Placement currently relies on compass-named dropdown positions and a panel covering the room. A future visual placement preview or highlighted destination could make decorating easier to understand; this screenshot review cannot establish whether other feedback exists after interaction.

Player benefit: owners can see where their furniture is and visitors can enjoy the collection without implied edit access. Remaining opportunity: make the selection and destination more visually immediate on small screens.

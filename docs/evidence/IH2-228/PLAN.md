# IH2-228 · Give waiting players a choice and discoveries a home

The existing workbench recommendation leads. A folded second option gives players a different useful activity when one is available. The choice avoids filling a short screen and stays stable while the bench timer updates. The selected route uses the existing destination and inspection path. Opening it leaves the job, held resources and permissions alone.

Riverwalk finds have a private illustrated section in the Journal as well as the river panel. The count and images come from the same confirmed browser record. A line that is out still has its own timer and walk back to the river. The section takes one line until opened and hides until the first find. Shared practice has no browser river record.

## Reference mechanics

- Stardew Valley's developer describes independent skills, exploration, expressions of home and town restoration. That suggests comparing whether a resident can choose a genuinely different nearby activity during a long bench job. [Developer overview](https://www.stardewvalley.net/about/)
- Nintendo describes automatically catalogued discoveries, optional museum displays and decorating at one's own pace. That suggests a discovery should remain readable after leaving its source, with visible expression as a later optional use. [Official New Horizons guide](https://animalcrossing.nintendo.com/new-horizons/explore/)

These are comparisons of documented mechanics. Browser runs measure choices, legibility, repeated navigation and preservation. Voluntary return and enjoyment still need human play observations. Evaluate whether players remember a particular find, can name another task during a wait, and voluntarily choose a second activity in an unprompted 10–20 minute session. A short follow-up after an offline return should ask what they intended to do and whether they found it. No attendance reward, urgency cue or player telemetry is added.

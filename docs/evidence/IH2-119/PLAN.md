# A first playable woodland outing

Queued after118, not implemented. The [November17 contract](https://github.com/dadbod87/idle-hands-2/blob/9cc9c52a94eb0928bcc148b146da6899039540c3/docs/idle-hands-2/design/OUTING_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) and [hands-on rehearsal](https://github.com/dadbod87/idle-hands-2/tree/2a398adb097fcc892cf8ffc0bcb3cf571c6832fe/tools/woodland-rehearsal) justify a limited actual-game prototype and Nick's local trial. They do not establish replay enjoyment, human cooperation or a finished three-to-five-minute session. Do not merge the design branch wholesale.

Use existing outskirts space before expanding the city again. Give the resident a clear way home, two readable route conditions, one visible hands-on repair and a private journal outcome. Borrowed brace/rope are temporary activity choices, distinct from owned equipment. Exchange freely, take an open detour or leave unfinished without losing ordinary progress. An unfinished departure is never a successful repair. Finishing must be possible alone with both production lanes busy, full stores and completed starter collections.

The first outcome grants no resources, existing practice, furnishing copy, extra capacity or town-restoration credit. A visible repair and journal record are the chosen proof. Later reward/skill/cooperation/pacing need separate ledgers and evidence; do not use repetition or a forced wait to manufacture depth.

Implement one coherent pure-state/persistence/authority/UI/art slice. Current local and signed shared paths require explicit forward evolution, all-writer/cached-client compatibility and exact preservation of old save fields, immutable migration bytes, jobs, gear and home allocations. A server-issued run/content version, trusted owner/location, same-ID payload replay before fresh revision/proximity refusal, semantic once-only completion, two-tab races and atomic journal/receipt commit need real tests. Reload/reconnect/real dedicated restart/dump-restore must preserve the run and old work. Animation follows confirmed state; the resettable rehearsal is not a durable save model.

Readable native desktop/phone/short/text200world interaction and keyboard/tap controls, safe door/return geometry, optional concise explanations, menu lock and Terra6.1 review are required. Keep current full source/CI/main/raw/original-preview gates. Record player benefit, frustration removed and what a human still needs to evaluate. No public/cloud launch, accounts/imports, paid assets, combat/chat or real-player changes.

## Play the encounter

Choose a practice resident. Find **The Pumpworks** in Places under **Outdoors & exploring**. At the Survey locker, take the sounding rod and one oatcake. The bow rewards standing still, the staff can push a vulnerable hopper, and the rod counters from close range. Try the practice bell on the dry side. Cross toward the chamber to draw the hopper. Move off the charge line, then strike while its shell opens. On a keyboard use Space, Shift and F. On a phone walk or drag and use the Strike, Brace and oatcake controls. Confirmed hits teach the equipped weapon. The bell gives feedback without skill credit.

The server derives contact from its own clock and confirmed feet. Active encounters belong to each resident. Entering together gives each visitor their own hopper. Damage, training and accepted result receipts share one private field record. A successful retry reuses its receipt. Disconnect or process restart retires the old encounter while keeping settled health and training. Defeat sends the resident to the dry bench, where they can recover, then return to lost property. The first field prototype uses this one bench as its recovery point. Read menus on the dry side. The hopper keeps moving during live combat, so an open panel closes if an encounter is active.

## Learn your trade by making something

- **Smithing.** The dry-side forge has one supplied practice blank. Make and collect a riveted vest after 30 real minutes to reach Rank 1 and get Armor 1. The follow-up brass plate takes 15 minutes and unlocks Rank 2 and a brass finish for the riveted vest. Apply the finish in Bag → Equipment. The field order uses supplied materials and has its own durable record.
- **Tailoring.** At the workshop sewing table, spend one Supplies on a trail kerchief. Collect after 10 real minutes for Rank 1. Gold edging uses one more Supplies and takes 15 minutes. Collect it for Rank 2 and the option to add gold edging to a dyed knot or wrap. You can style or pack the kerchief from Bag after collection. The ordinary bench runs alongside these sewing orders.
- **Keep your style.** Dyes, finishes, packing and wearing preserve rank. A neighbor sees the actual worn vest and kerchief together, and can inspect them in Nearby outfits. That projection contains only visible clothing. Training, materials, packed items and active combat stay private.
- **Come back when ready.** The Journal shows the current orders, lights when work is ready, and walks to the appropriate station. A player can gather materials or explore while an order runs. The server's deadline determines collection.

The two techniques per trade are the first playable ranks. Future patterns can add harder work without granting repeated credit for applying an existing finish. The matching armor and cloth preview helps players see what they earned and try a different outfit before confirming.

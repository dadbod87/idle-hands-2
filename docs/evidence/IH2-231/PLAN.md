# IH2-231 · Put finished work first on return

The Journal brings an already-finished crop, bench job, finishing job, orchard parcel or cast to the front when opened. Waiting work, first-morning directions and other notes stay available. The existing entry opens the same inspector or walk. No separate claim button, timer, polling, confirmation or reward is introduced.

Ordering is chosen on open from the current confirmed state. While the Journal is open, timers and status still change in place. If a job finishes under a finger or mouse, targets and focus remain in the same positions. Produce or snack storage that is full stays identified as needing room. Shared Workshop notes stay tied to the current confirmed resident and the usual guarded inspector.

The combined local case uses valid elapsed garden and orchard saves created with ordinary pure commands. It verifies one entry for each, a ready garden before waiting work, no horizontal overflow, the close control, and byte-identical records after a touch or keyboard transition on desktop, 320px phone and enlarged short landscape. The original held-mouse expiry case uses the real five-second teaching job and continues to protect geometry and focus. The shared confirmed-notes case uses fictional intercepted responses.

# IH2-099 café feasibility critique

A finite hospitality chapter using existing harvested produce is the strongest candidate for a *study*, provided the action has a visible purpose and the team accepts that it is a short optional chapter, not sustained active play. A two-minute preparation is another wait unless the player can make a meaningful choice or do a distinct activity during it; the proposed shared opening and permanent personal keepsake provide purpose, but their thresholds and timing are unapproved hypotheses. Prototype the decision and its consequences before selecting values. Scenery/dialogue alone gives atmosphere without a resource loop; it is preferable to shipping a hollow timer if the chapter cannot be made clear. A new herb/soup ledger is premature: seed entitlement, third-bed pressure, storage, food use and value are unresolved, and an unused edible stack compounds inventory without purpose.

## Scarcity and choice

Source confirms two bound radish seeds, one marigold seed and three beds; first radish takes five minutes and later radishes 30 minutes, marigold four hours. Harvest returns the seed and yields one produce, capped at 12; a full stack keeps the plant and seed waiting. One radish can therefore compete directly with Pip's once-only letterbox (2 pine + 1 harvested radish); two radishes delay a café-first player until another harvest. Show the explicit cost and, when Pip's box is unmade, a short contextual reminder that it also needs a radish. Offer a choice rather than reserving produce automatically. Avoid marigold for an introductory café step: the easel requires one harvested flower and the existing goal guard on hamper deliveries is meaningful; the mixed request also spends a flower. Any hypothetical café debit requires a new conservation category/migration rather than silently reusing an unrelated counter.

## Smallest coherent study

One counter card could show “Tasting for the café,” held/required harvested radish, exact proposed preparation time, and the current next action: Preview → named confirmation → preparing → ready to Serve. Keep cancel free, explicit collection/serving, a stable result region and a visible destination. Separate “town opening” from the personal keepsake: the public threshold is capped, but a serve after opening still counts toward the same personal entitlement. Make the post-completion state honest: if it offers recognition only, say so before the player spends produce; consider whether a no-benefit repeat is necessary at all. A cooldown beginning at Serve is understandable if shown as a real deadline, but evaluate the interaction of a held ready job and cooldown, repeated service after completion, and whether a 2-minute wait plus a 4-hour lock feels like useful activity. No automatic repeat, stock drain, or lost ready output.

## Experiment before balance

Use a deterministic simulation/worksheet with clearly labeled candidate values, not a runtime commitment. Compare café-first and Pip-first new residents from ordinary starter seeds; goal-first easel vs café/hamper/request choices; solo resident absent longer than a cooldown; three residents racing the last public credit; late newcomer after opening; player already owning reward; full result/storage and lost-reply/replay. Track earliest possible decision and completion times, actual produce consumed and remaining, seeds/beds tied up, public credit cap, personal entitlement and no-spend/no-loss outcomes. Include 1 vs 2 radish and no-timer control. Ask whether each state offers a meaningful choice or merely a wait; a simulated timeline cannot establish fun or retention.

If later implemented, require versioned state/conservation migration, authoritative proximity/identity, atomic capped public credit and once-only personal award, idempotent retry, no lost output when storage is full, and existing work/property preservation. None of those mechanics or candidate thresholds is approved here. This review is source-informed planning, not gameplay or human playtesting.

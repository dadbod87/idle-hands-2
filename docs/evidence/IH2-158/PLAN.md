# Give the resident an optional arrival story

Choose an office transfer, independent maker, or returning local in the existing resident editor. The boarding-house note reflects the saved choice. A new Walk to Marlow button follows the actual door and street route to the clerk. Step outside remains available for players who want to explore alone.

Story changes stay in browser appearance data. The existing name, look, headwear, workshop, and position are preserved. Version 1 and 2 profiles keep their exact format until a story is explicitly saved. Clearing the story returns to the matching prior format. A cancelled choice changes nothing. A stale tab or an unknown story cannot overwrite the current record.

Two additional domain checks cover preserving, clearing, and rejecting choices. Six focused native cases cover both old profiles and the new editable story, reload, walking to the clerk, the existing headwear, and the boarding house. Narrow and enlarged short layouts keep the selected value and both actions visible. Terra caught a clipped dropdown at 200% text. The short editor is wider now, and a rendered-text check verifies the selected label fits. Tests use fictional browser records without a game API. Physical devices and shared identities are separate.

A future first-morning flow can use this choice and the real safe room. It still needs a single confirmed wake checkpoint, opt-in eligibility, and recovery on reload before it can become a start flow.

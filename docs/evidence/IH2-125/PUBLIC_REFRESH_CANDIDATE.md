## Question and benefit

When many residents contribute to one public project, how many invalidation messages, snapshot reads and queued connections result? Are real clients keeping menus responsive and eventually showing the final committed public state without losing private progress? Answer before adding caches, polling, a larger pool or infrastructure. A calmer shared town and truthful feedback are the player benefit; database-query count alone is insufficient.

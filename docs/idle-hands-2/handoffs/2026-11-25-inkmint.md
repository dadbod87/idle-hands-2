# November 25 — first Inkmint finish

## What a resident can do

After the Glasshouse opens, choose either keepsake plant from the keeper. The seed drawers offer one free Inkmint tray. It ripens in 24 real hours and waits for an explicit harvest. The Journal shows the due time, marks it ready and offers a walk to the drawers. One harvest makes one pigment. Take it to the pottery ledge and mix the stripe, then apply it at your own windowsill. You can restore the original pot and reapply the stripe. The new finish changes the pot body while keeping the plant, rim, labels and silhouette. The plant remains private until the owner explicitly displays it. A visitor in that home sees finish changes without closing the inspection.

This trial has one crop and one finish. Existing production clocks, stock, placements and scores use their existing rules. It does not introduce trade, paid recoloring, repeating harvests or a deadline for collecting a ripe crop. The standalone Swatch Book remains a projection of possible breadth. Try this small live candidate before adding colors or another crafting gate.

# IH2-236 · Inspect the displayed print

A resident can bring a river find or the pressed marigold into the workshop and choose one for the stand. Tap the physical frame, or walk to Workshop print through Places and inspect it when nearby. The printed story and picture are also readable through keyboard controls. Taking a print down removes the optional destination immediately. The linked garden button opens the earned print's preview and leaves the decision to display it with the player.

The same supported browser preference keeps older Riverwalk prints. Garden, river, placement and bench progress stay intact while residents browse, hang or remove art. The display is opt-in and private to this browser. Workshop collision and shared authority stay in their existing paths.

The local browser tests use a fictional saved garden with one matured marigold, earned river records, and a controlled print-storage failure. They exercise actual client rendering, walking, harvesting, selecting, displaying, inspecting, removal and reload. Terra examined WebKit desktop, phone and short enlarged captures. Follow-up changes enlarge the select, keep the confirmation in view and open the marigold page directly from the allotment.

# Sound options placement: visual planning

The desktop square views show a location/weather card at the upper left of the world, with room across its top edge; the header already carries the guide button. The phone cottage view has a narrow header with resident and guide controls, a location card below at left, and a bottom row occupied by Places, Bag, Journal and contextual interaction. The short/enlarged cottage view has particularly little world height and a full bottom row. The supplied rain phone image is covered by a depot dialog, so it does not establish unobscured rainy-world placement.

Recommend a single 44px Sound options icon button at the upper right of the world, with an accessible name and clear silent-state icon. Reserve its space independently of the left location/weather card, including when that card wraps; keep the same world overlay position across outdoor and indoor scenes. This preserves the crowded phone header and bottom actions and avoids consuming a permanent extra row. At narrow widths or 200% text, constrain the location card to the remaining width and allow its content to wrap without overlapping the button; test long location/resident/weather strings and short viewports. Avoid moving the resident, guide, bottom controls or interaction targets. A brief first-use label or other non-covering cue could improve icon discoverability, but should not occupy the playfield permanently.

The proposed dialog should provide deliberate Enable/Resume, Pause/Mute and separate music/effects levels, initially silent. These are design recommendations, not a verified layout: stills do not prove available space for every state, keyboard/touch operation, scroll behavior or physical-device usability. Validate the proposed button and dialog at narrow, short and enlarged sizes before integration.

# November 23 · Small routines for the town regulars

Marlow checks a clipboard, Pip lifts a sealed letter and Jun checks a seed packet. The original prop silhouettes use the existing ink, paper and brass colors. They animate briefly in a staggered cycle and step out under reduced motion. The actors retain their feet, hit regions, dialogue and paths. Each prop is drawn once by the game, with no new image requests or recurring game work.

The client compiled and the design check passed. Chromium and WebKit each passed the existing ambient reset case and a new actual-canvas case. The latter captured all three routines from a fresh desktop arrival, Pip at 320px touch, and opened Pip’s normal dialogue while the prop was visible. The browser-local stock and jobs stayed identical and no API call was made. Device timings and fuller gesture poses remain later work.

## Progression and choice results

Daily visits are one 10-minute window; regular visits are two 15-minute windows; active visits are four 30-minute windows; frequent visits are twelve five-minute windows. Absence variants omit days 14–20. These are declared conditional schedules, not forecasts of real player behavior or phone travel times.

All 48 paired optional-letterbox comparisons preserve the complete ordinary kit claim series. Housing eligibility is only a conditional cumulative held-kit budget: 12 kits around day 12 for daily returns and day 6 for twice-daily or more; 36 kits around day 36/day 18 respectively, with absence delaying some beyond the 42-day observation. Models spend no kits on plots, upgrades, maintenance or donations and do not simulate SQL land competition. These are not house-purchase dates.

Letterbox, easel, hamper and request choices genuinely compete for pine/produce. A box can delay the sixth optional kept-craft record by up to 12 hours in the tested windows and yield up to two fewer hampers over six weeks. All 24 civic-first cohorts continuously spend eligible flowers and produce no kept easel; an after-first-kept box therefore remains unmade. This is a voluntary priority outcome, not a broken rule. Easel-first reserves ingredients through six started easels. Request-aware serves an eligible request first while continuing crafting when a request is blocked. Both latter policies voluntarily stop request/easel goals at six. Permanent repeatable new content is not inferred from these finite goals.

Already-funded newcomers still have their own first planter, upkeep recognition, open trail/discoveries, crops/errand/markers, sketchbook, pine tools/easel and Pip route/letterbox. No private starting stock or plot discount is awarded. In all three observed widths Drew's trail is open, the free addressed chapter is available and the 12-kit house claim correctly remains unaffordable. Shared project completion is finite; it does not create endless new cooperation.

Owner/visitor expression works in the observed loop. Six distinct furnishings remain recognizable, visitor controls explicitly deny edits, moving the one letterbox empties its previous nook, unpublished placed motifs update for visitors, optional public samples can be added/removed, and reload preserves ownership. Register samples do not create physical copies. [Phone home](screens/phone-owned-home-world.png).

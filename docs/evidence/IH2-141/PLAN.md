# Make the orchard a useful outing

The eastern orchard is already visible on the map and reachable by ordinary travel. Its apples are currently decorative. The design catalog already names orchard fruit and a prepared snack. This rehearsal selects a small useful connection between them before broader crop species, food effects or shared orchard construction.

## Candidate play

- Inspect three existing trees near Orchard walk. A ready branch offers one apple. Harvesting uses that resident's entitlement, so a coworker cannot strip the orchard for everyone. When a branch regrows, the next apple waits. Long absence carries no loss.
- Carry up to six apples. Combine two into one packed snack in its own small preparation slot. A candidate thirty real minutes lets the resident start the task and continue garden, workshop, delivery or exploration. Pickups are explicit. A full basket or snack stack preserves the outstanding thing.
- Keep the apple and folded snack visually distinct. A later playable slice needs original art for ready/regrowing and reach/contact/recovery, one short receipt, and low-motion alternatives. Avoid marking a public canopy empty because one resident picked it.

## Evidence and next gate

[Observation summary](OBSERVATIONS.json) compares visits every 8, 12 and 24 hours with 6, 8 and 12 hour regrowth. Five pure tests cover collision-safe approach, personal and exact ripeness, stock and reservation conservation, offline collection, and a model demand of one snack per day. With no use, all schedules fill at six apples and three snacks in the first week. With illustrative daily use, each sustains six or seven tastes. All nine combinations reach their first completed snack on the next visit. More frequent visits do not create unlimited score, skills or housing progress. They can bank more snacks sooner. The three native route calculations span around 6 to 11 tiles from the orchard sign. These are comparisons on modeled schedules and actual map geometry, without measured human play.

Use eight hours per branch as a provisional starting value for a later candidate. This test gives no strong reason to increase supply. Before a playable release, decide a useful actual food action and test its economic boundaries. A quiet first tasting can precede combat. Recovery effects, prices, shared restoration, player orchards and new sources need their own verified rules. No health system or resource acquisition has been enabled here. The trial also does not move the future shared restoration or town opening decision.

Why this layout?

Homes face clear public lanes. A central walk connects the gate, all four streets and the commons. Doors and arrival points stay outside building collision.

New blocks preserve existing addresses. Invite order must not leave someone without a home.

Artwork here reuses the two accepted prototype houses. Exterior variety, player names, visitors and room transitions require the next implementation slice.

Town plan

Ledgerwick keeps work, post, gardens and the town project near the square. Homes have their own quieter streets. Inkwood remains a distinct woodland route; Mossbank and Bellwether Quay are later settlements with their own materials and services.
